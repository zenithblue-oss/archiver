Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Archiver is a privacy-first archive utility for iOS, iPadOS, macOS, and visionOS maintained by ZenithBlue. This policy describes how Archiver handles your data.

Zero Data Collection

Archiver is built with complete user privacy as a foundational principle:

  • No Analytics or Telemetry: Archiver contains zero tracking SDKs, advertising libraries, third-party analytics, or behavioral telemetry frameworks.
  • No Remote Servers: Archiver operates without online accounts or logins. The application does not transmit your archives, files, directory names, or passwords to any remote server.
  • Fully Offline Processing: All compression, extraction, verification, and inspection routines execute locally on your physical hardware.

Local Storage & Security

  • File Access: Archiver strictly uses Apple's security-scoped bookmarks and system document pickers. It accesses only the individual files and folders you explicitly choose to open.
  • Password Vault: Passwords stored in the built-in vault are encrypted and saved securely inside Apple's Keychain on your device.
  • iCloud Synchronization (Optional): If you enable iCloud sync, your preferences and encrypted vault items sync exclusively across your own devices through Apple's end-to-end encrypted iCloud / private CloudKit database. The developer has no access to your iCloud data.

System Permissions

  • File Storage: Used only to read archives you select and save extracted outputs to destinations you specify.
  • Photo Library (Optional): Requested only if you explicitly choose to save an extracted picture directly to your Photos library.

App Store Privacy Nutrition Label

Under Apple's App Store privacy guidelines, Archiver's privacy label is Data Not Collected.
