Features

Everything an archive tool should do

Free download. An optional one-time Pro purchase adds AES-256 archive creation, more than 3 presets, and batch extraction. Everything else is free.

Open and extract

  • ZIP, 7Z, RAR, TAR, GZ, BZ2, XZ, ZSTD, Brotli, ISO, CAB, WIM, and more
  • Multi-volume and split archives (.z01, .7z.001, .part1.rar)
  • Opens AES-256 and legacy ZipCrypto archives

Preview without a full extract

  • Browse files, folders, sizes, and compression ratios
  • Preview images, documents, audio, and text in place
  • Quick Look and thumbnails in Files and Finder

  • ZIP, 7Z, TAR, TAR.GZ, TAR.BZ2, TAR.XZ, TAR.ZST, TAR.BR, TAR.LZ4, XAR, and ISO
  • Password-protected ZIP and 7Z with AES-256 PRO
  • 7Z can also encrypt file names

Deep platform integration

Privacy

Data not collected. Ever.

All archive work happens on your device. Archiver has no analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts, and no developer servers.

Read the privacy policy

  • On-device onlyYour files never leave your iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Vision Pro.
  • Keychain password vaultArchive passwords live in the Apple Keychain. iCloud Keychain sync is optional and off by default.
  • You choose the foldersArchiver only sees folders you grant. Revoke access any time.
